
## Teaching at U Michigan
- <strong>ME 382 Mechanical Behavior of Materials</strong> (F25, F26)
    - **Some web resources: lm3-lab.github.io/ME382**
    - Comprehensive overview of materials science topics for mechanical engineers 
    - Around 100 students (half of total intake, core course for major)
    - <a href="https://me.engin.umich.edu/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/ME-382-Course-Profile.pdf" target="_new">UM Course Profile</a>

- <strong>ME 507 Atomistic Simulation of Materials</strong> (W26, W27)
    - Graduate level course, around 20-30 students
    - Coding assignments expose students to state-of-the-art
    - Particular deep dive on ML interatomic potentials, enhanced sampling and informatics
    - <a href="./assets/pdf/ME507_Syllabus.pdf" target="_new">Syllabus for Winter 27 term</a>

- <strong>COMPLIT 394: Opening the black box of machine translation</strong> (W27)
    - Expanded set of lectures on machine translation for the comparative literature department
    - Dual-taught course, roughly 50:50 split between practice in machine and human translation 
    - History (Georgetown, Babelfish, n-grams) and present (transformers, mBERT, LLMs)
    - Focus on demystification of LLM-based tools, and fundamental limitations (context, tails)
    - Introduction to fine-tuning / pre-prompting for targeted behavior with open weight models.

- <strong>COMPLIT 200: Introduction to translation</strong> (F25, W26)
    - Four lecture mini-course on machine translation in the comparative literature department, around 30 students
    - History (Georgetown, Babelfish, n-grams) and present (transformers, mBERT)
    - Focus on demystification of LLM-based tools, and fundamental limitations (context, tails)
